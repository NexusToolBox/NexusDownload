# Code of conduct

Be respectful, discuss ideas constructively, and protect other people’s privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination and publication of private information are unacceptable. Report concerns in this repository’s [Issues][issues] without repeating private material. Maintainers may remove harmful content or limit participation.
