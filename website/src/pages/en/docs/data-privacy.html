Data & privacy

Backup and migration

History, settings and account details live in the data folder beside the app. Keep it when moving the app. Back up downloaded files separately if they are saved elsewhere.

Sign in to your cloud drives again after moving to a different computer or Windows user.

Uninstall

Quit the app and delete its folder. Back up any data and downloaded files you want to keep first.
